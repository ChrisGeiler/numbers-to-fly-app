Numbers to Fly

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 August 2026

Numbers to Fly is a private performance wingsuiting application. This policy explains what information the app handles, why it is used, and the choices available to approved users.

Information the app handles

  • Account information supplied during sign-in, such as your email address and, when provided by Google, basic profile information.
  • Access-control information, including the approved email list and whether an account is an owner or an allowed user.
  • FlySight CSV data you choose to import. A track can include timestamps, precise location, altitude, speed, and related GPS measurements.
  • Logbook details you choose to save, such as task type, date, location name, suit, notes, calculated results, coordinates, and the imported CSV.
  • Body details and preferences you choose to save for the Find your Numbers calculator, including weight, height, unit system, and suit setup.

How information is used

Information is used to sign you in, confirm that your email has been approved, calculate and display flight-performance results, and provide your private logbook. Saved calculator details are used to prefill the Find your Numbers page for your account. Information is not used for advertising and is not sold.

Storage and service providers

The app uses Supabase for authentication and app data. Google may process account information when you choose Google sign-in. The website is delivered through GitHub Pages. These providers process information under their own terms and privacy policies.

Sharing

Personal information is not shared with other app users as a normal feature. It may be processed by the service providers needed to run the app, or disclosed when required by law.

Retention and your choices

Saved data is retained while it is needed to provide the app. You can delete saved jumps in the app. The owner can remove an email from the access list. You can also request account or associated data deletion by contacting the address below.

Security

Access is limited to approved email addresses and data access is protected through the app's authentication and database controls. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the app or its data practices change. The date at the top of this page will show the latest revision.

Contact

For privacy questions or deletion requests, email flywithcruza@gmail.com.
